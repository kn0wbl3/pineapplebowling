## When and Where

* **Wednesdays at 6:30 PM**
* **[Bowlero White Plains](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bowlero+White+Plains)**
* **2027 season:** January 6 to April 28 (15 weeks). See the [schedule](schedule) for off weeks and position nights.
